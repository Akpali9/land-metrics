Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated 11 November 2026

1. Who we are

LAND METRICS (“we”, “us”) runs the LAND METRICS mobile app, a marketplace where people list, buy and sell land and property, request land verification and apply for construction. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

Contact: support@yourdomain.com Address: Your registered address, Nigeria

2. Information we collect

Account details: your name, phone number, email address, password (stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider) and optional profile picture.

Listings: titles, locations, sizes, prices, descriptions and photos you upload.

Transactions: payment receipts you upload, amounts, transfer references and the status of your purchases and sales. We never collect card numbers or bank passwords.

Service documents: land titles, identification, survey plans and building plans you upload when you request verification or construction.

Communications: messages you send to our admins in the app, and your support and service requests.

Technical data: a device token used to send push notifications, crash and performance reports, and basic device and app version information.

AI features: text you type into the AI assistant and the listing details you ask the AI to write about.

3. How we use your information

To create and secure your account; to publish and review listings; to verify payments; to provide verification and construction services; to send you notifications about your listings, payments and requests; to prevent fraud and abuse; to provide support; to fix bugs and improve the app; and to meet our legal obligations.

4. Our legal bases

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 we rely on: performance of our contract with you; our legitimate interests in running a safe marketplace and fixing errors; your consent where we ask for it (for example push notifications); and legal obligations such as financial record keeping.

5. Who we share it with

Other users can see your listing details (title, location, price, photos and description). We do not show your name, phone number or email to other users.

Our admins can see your account details, listings, receipts, requests and chat messages in order to run the service.

We use service providers who process data for us: Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage and server functions); Anthropic (AI responses); Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications); and Sentry (crash reports). They may only use data to provide their services to us.

We do not sell your personal data. We may disclose data when the law requires it or to protect people from fraud or harm.

6. International transfers

Some providers process data outside Nigeria. Where this happens we use providers that apply appropriate safeguards and we limit what we send.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your profile details, messages, requests, uploaded documents and device tokens. We keep payment and sale records for as long as financial and tax laws require, and we keep them without your name and contact details where possible.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, and receive a copy of your data. You can edit your profile and delete your account inside the app (Profile > Delete my account). For other requests, email support@yourdomain.com. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls and private storage for sensitive documents. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private. We will never ask for your password.

10. AI features

Messages you send to the AI assistant and listing details you send to “Write with AI” are processed by our AI provider to produce a reply. Please do not enter identity numbers, bank details or other sensitive information. AI answers can be wrong and are not legal or financial advice.

11. Children

LAND METRICS is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. If we make important changes we will notify you in the app. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@yourdomain.com
